# Privacy Policy

**App:** Focus Timer Desk Clock  
**Effective date:** August 25, 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how WBB ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Focus Timer Desk Clock application (the "App") on iOS or Android devices.

We respect your privacy. Please read this policy carefully. If you do not agree with the relevant practices, stop using those features or uninstall the App.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to processing carried out through the version of the App distributed on the Apple App Store and Google Play, and future updates, to the extent of features actually enabled on your device.

## 3. Information We May Process

Depending on how you use the App and your device settings, the following types of information may be processed:

### 3.1 On-device (local) data

The App may store on your device information such as:

- Timer settings, durations, display options, and interface preferences
- Other app options you configure
- Cached data or session state needed for the App to function

This data stays on your device. It is not sent to our servers — we do not operate a server for the App — unless we introduce sync or backend API features in the future and clearly disclose that in the App and in this Policy. See [Data Retention and Deletion](#7-data-retention-and-deletion) for how long it is kept and how to delete it.

### 3.2 Focus session history and statistics

The App records your completed and interrupted focus sessions so that it can show you reports, statistics, and charts about your own focus habits. This history is built up automatically as you use the App and may include:

- Start time, end time, and duration of each session
- Session type (for example focus, short break, long break) and whether it was completed or stopped early
- Counts and totals derived from the above (daily, weekly, and longer-term streaks, totals, and averages)
- Any optional label, task name, or note **you choose to type yourself** for a session

**This history is stored only in local storage on your own device.** It is not uploaded to us, it is not shared with any third party, and it is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than displaying your own reports inside the App. We have no server and therefore no way to see it.

Because a label or note is free text, please avoid typing information you would not want stored on your device — the App does not need it, and the statistics work without it.

See [Data Retention and Deletion](#7-data-retention-and-deletion) for how long this history is kept and how to delete it.

### 3.3 In-app purchase (IAP) data

When you buy a paid product or subscription in the App, Apple or Google processes payment and provides the App with transaction or entitlement status (for example which plan is active) through each store's mechanisms. We do not receive your full card number; payment details are handled by Apple/Google under their own policies.

### 3.4 Technical and device data

When the App uses the network (if applicable), servers or third-party services may receive typical technical information, such as:

- Device type and operating system version
- Preferred language / locale
- Data needed to load content (for example images or animations) or call APIs

**Advertising identifiers:** If the App does not integrate third-party advertising SDKs to track you for ads, we do not collect advertising identifiers for that purpose. If that changes, we will update this Policy and any required in-app or store disclosures.

### 3.5 Sensors and system permissions

The App may use device capabilities only when you grant permission and a feature actually needs them (for example motion/accelerometer for desk mode, screen brightness where the OS allows).

:::note
Store build configuration may declare additional permissions (for example camera, location, photo library). This Policy describes data collected through features actually in use. If the App does not use a declared permission, the publisher should update store permission disclosures to match reality and stay consistent with Apple App Privacy and Google Play Data safety.
:::

## 4. How We Use Information

We process information to:

- Comply with legal obligations

**We do not sell your personal information.**

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

**We do not operate any server or backend for the App, and we do not collect, store, or retain any user data on our systems.** All data created by the App — your timer settings, durations, display options, interface preferences, and session state — is stored **only in local storage on your own device**. It is never uploaded to us, and we have no ability to access it.

### 7.1 Retention periods

| Data | Where it is stored | How long it is retained |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Timer settings, durations, display and interface preferences | Locally on your device only | Until you delete it in the App, clear the App's data in your device settings, or uninstall the App. We retain **no copy**, and there is **no retention period on our side**. |
| Focus session history and the statistics, reports, and charts derived from it (Section 3.2) | Locally on your device only | Retained on your device **indefinitely**, so that long-term reports and streaks stay available to you, until you delete it. We retain **no copy**, and there is **no retention period on our side** because the data never reaches us. |
| Cached data and session state | Locally on your device only | Overwritten by the App as you use it, and removed entirely when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. We retain **no copy**. |
| In-app purchase / subscription records | Held by Apple and Google, not by us | Retained by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. We only read the current entitlement status from the store at runtime and **do not store it**. |
| Technical request data (device type, OS version, locale) sent when the App loads remote content, if applicable | Transient, in the logs of the hosting or store provider | Not retained by us. Provider logs expire on that provider's own schedule. |

We do not maintain user accounts, user profiles, analytics profiles, advertising identifiers, or server-side backups of your data. Because nothing is retained on our systems, there is nothing on our side to expire, archive, or delete.

### 7.2 How to delete your data

You are in full control of every piece of data the App creates, and you can delete it at any time without contacting us:

- **iOS:** delete the App from your Home Screen or via *Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Focus Timer Desk Clock → Delete App*.
- **Android:** *Settings → Apps → Focus Timer Desk Clock → Storage → Clear data* (or uninstall the App).
- **Inside the App:** reset your timer and display preferences back to their defaults from the App's settings screen.

Clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App permanently deletes **all** locally stored App data, including your entire focus session history and every statistic, report, and chart derived from it. This cannot be undone, and we cannot restore it for you because we never held a copy.

If you ever have a question about deletion, or believe we hold data about you, email us at [vincenttrandeveloper@gmail.com](mailto:vincenttrandeveloper@gmail.com) with the subject line **"Data Deletion Request"**. We respond within **30 days**. Purchase and subscription records are held by Apple or Google and must be requested from them directly, under their own policies.

If we ever introduce sync, accounts, or any other server-side feature, we will publish the specific retention periods for that data in this Policy and update the store Data safety disclosures **before** the feature is enabled.

### 7.3 Device backups

Your device's own backup service may include the App's local data — and therefore your focus session history — in your device backup. On iOS this is iCloud Backup; on Android it is Google's Auto Backup for Apps. These backups are created and controlled by Apple or Google under **your** account and **their** privacy and retention policies, not ours: we never receive them and cannot read them.

You can control this in your device settings — on iOS via *Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage* and on Android via *Settings → Google → Backup* — and a backup copy is removed when you delete the backup or turn the service off for the App.
