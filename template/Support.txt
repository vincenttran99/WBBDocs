Decibel Measurement
The App measures noise levels using your device's built-in microphone. Readings are uncalibrated estimates and are intended for personal documentation purposes only — they are not certified legal-grade acoustic measurements. Accuracy may vary between devices. You can adjust the calibration offset in Settings to improve accuracy for your specific device.

Noise Law Data
The noise law database is pre-loaded and works offline. Data covers jurisdictions across the US, Germany, Japan, South Korea, UK, France, Singapore, and Australia, and is provided for general reference. Always verify current regulations with local authorities or a qualified professional before taking legal action.

PDF & ZIP Export
PDF report and ZIP evidence bundle export are Premium features. If export is unavailable, check your subscription status in Settings → Restore Purchases. Reports are generated entirely on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.
